A GOOD PLACE TO START

Insurance, in everyday language.

Open a guide to explore the basics. General information only; policy terms and benefits vary.

Life and health

Term vs. health insurance

What each type is designed to cover.

Term cover may provide a benefit to your chosen beneficiaries if you die during the policy term. Health cover helps with eligible medical expenses, subject to policy limits, terms and exclusions. They address different needs.
Plain-language guide

Insurance words, decoded

Premium, sum insured and waiting period.

Premium: the amount paid for a policy. Sum insured: the stated health-cover limit. Waiting period: a period before specified benefits may apply. Check the policy wording for the exact meaning.
Before you buy

A policy comparison checklist

Key details to look at before choosing.

Compare who is covered, exclusions, waiting periods, claim requirements, renewal conditions and total cost. Tax treatment depends on current rules and your situation; check with a qualified tax professional.
